# 04 — Repair, Appreciation & Offline Suggestions

The features that keep Coupling from becoming "the fight app" — repair-oriented
communication, positive communication, and knowing when to leave the app entirely.

---

## 11. Repair Mode

The app supports repair-oriented communication.

**Repair features:**

- Apology drafting
- Clarification drafting
- Acknowledgment drafting
- De-escalation response
- "I said that badly" message
- Pause request
- Common-ground summary
- Follow-up after conflict
- Appreciation after repair

This should be core, not secondary.

---

## 12. Appreciation and Positive Communication

Coupling should not only be used during conflict.

**It should support:**

- Appreciation
- Affection
- Gratitude
- Encouragement
- Celebration
- Reconnection
- Support during stress
- Planning positive events

This keeps the app from becoming "the fight app."

---

## 13. Offline Action Suggestions

The app may suggest that a conversation move offline.

**Examples:**

- "This may be better as a voice conversation."
- "This looks circular. Consider pausing and returning later."
- "This seems important enough for an in-person conversation."
- "You may want to take 20 minutes before replying."
- "This may require professional support outside the app."

The app should suggest, not force, unless safety mode is triggered.
